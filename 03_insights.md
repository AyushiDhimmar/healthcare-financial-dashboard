# 💡 Key Business Insights — Healthcare Financial Performance

**Author:** Ayushi Dhimmar  
**Period:** 2023–2024 | **Records:** 500 | **Total Revenue:** ₹33.0M

---

## 📊 Executive Summary

The healthcare financial data across 4 regions, 6 departments, and 5 treatment types reveals strong overall profitability (~29.8% margin), with significant variation across departments and regions. Revenue grew by 1.3% from 2023 to 2024, indicating steady but slow growth.

---

## 🔑 Top Insights

### 1. South Region Leads Revenue
- South generated **₹8.8M** — the highest among all regions
- West and East are close behind at ₹8.2M each
- North trails at ₹7.7M — a potential growth opportunity

### 2. General Medicine & Oncology Are Most Profitable
- **General Medicine:** 30.5% profit margin (highest)
- **Oncology:** 30.4% profit margin
- **Cardiology:** 27.9% profit margin (lowest) — high costs relative to revenue

### 3. Surgery & Medication Serve the Most Patients
- Surgery: **2,792 patients** | Medication: **2,786 patients**
- Diagnostics serves the fewest: **1,996 patients**
- High patient volume in Surgery suggests it's a core revenue driver

### 4. Revenue Growth Is Positive but Slow
| Year | Total Revenue |
|------|--------------|
| 2023 | ₹16.39M |
| 2024 | ₹16.61M |
| Growth | +1.3% |

### 5. Online Payment Dominates
- Online Payment: **182 records** (36.4%)
- Cash: **171 records** (34.2%)
- Insurance: **147 records** (29.4%)
- Opportunity: Push Insurance adoption — lower collection risk

### 6. Cardiology Has Low Margin Despite High Revenue
- Cardiology generates high revenue but has the lowest profit margin (27.9%)
- High treatment costs likely the cause
- **Recommendation:** Review Cardiology cost structure

### 7. Profit Margins Are Consistent Across Regions
- All regions maintain margins between 28–31%
- No single region is underperforming significantly
- Indicates good operational consistency

---

## ⚠️ Areas of Concern

| Area | Issue | Recommendation |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Cardiology | Lowest profit margin | Cost optimization needed |
| North Region | Lowest revenue | Expand patient outreach |
| Insurance Payments | Lowest adoption | Increase insurance tie-ups |
| Revenue Growth | Only 1.3% YoY | Set higher growth targets |

---

## ✅ Strengths

- No missing data — clean, reliable dataset
- Consistent profitability across all departments
- Diverse treatment portfolio (5 types)
- Multi-region presence (4 regions)

---

## 📌 Recommendations

1. **Focus on North region** — lowest revenue, needs targeted campaigns
2. **Optimize Cardiology costs** — bring margin in line with other departments
3. **Promote Insurance-based payments** — reduces cash flow risk
4. **Expand high-margin services** — scale General Medicine and Oncology
5. **Track monthly trends** — 2023–2024 data is promising, monitor Q-o-Q going forward
